ris

Browse the web with your eyes and voice.

A Chrome extension for people who cannot use a mouse or keyboard. Look at a link, blink twice, and it opens.

Look

Iris numbers every link and button. Your gaze snaps to the nearest one, so a normal webcam is enough.

Blink twice

A deliberate double blink opens it. Looking alone never clicks.

Speak

Say "open the history section" or "summarize this". Iris finds it; you confirm.

Looking

Your camera stays yours.

Video and face tracking run on your laptop and never leave it. Your voice goes out only during a conversation, and a page only when you ask about it.
