ris

Browse the web with your eyes and voice.

People with ALS, spinal cord injuries or cerebral palsy often cannot use a mouse or keyboard. Iris is a Chrome extension that lets them browse real websites hands-free.

Three ways in

Your face and eyes stay on your laptop. Only what you ask for leaves it.

  1. 01

    Look, then blink twice.

    The webcam follows your gaze and snaps it to the nearest link. Two blinks open it. Nothing clicks from gaze alone.

  2. 02

    Say where to go.

    "Scroll down." "Go back." "Open the history section." Iris finds the target on the page and moves there.

  3. 03

    Ask, and hear it back.

    Ask Iris to summarize a page or describe an image. It reads the answer aloud, in a voice, not a wall of text.
